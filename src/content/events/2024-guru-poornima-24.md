Guru Poornima 2024 was celebrated at Fankaar as a meaningful occasion dedicated to honouring the revered Guru-Shishya tradition. Fankaar students came together to express their respect and gratitude towards their Gurus through a curated presentation of dance and music.

The celebration featured Guru Vandana and Kathak dance presentations, reflecting the students’ dedication to their art and the values imparted by their Gurus. The occasion highlighted the significance of guidance, knowledge and tradition in shaping an artist’s journey.
