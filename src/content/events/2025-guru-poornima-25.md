Guru Poornima at Fankaar on 10 July 2025 was a celebration of gratitude and reverence for the Gurus who illuminate the path of artistic learning. The occasion recognised the profound relationship between Guru and disciple and the wisdom that shapes an artist’s journey.

With dance, music and moments of collective reflection, the celebration became a meaningful tribute to the traditions and teachings carried forward through generations. It was a day to acknowledge the role of the Guru in nurturing not just artistic expression, but also discipline, dedication and values.
