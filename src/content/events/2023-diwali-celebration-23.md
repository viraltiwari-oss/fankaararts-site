Fankaar Institute of Performing Arts celebrated Diwali on 14 November 2023 with warmth, joy and a strong sense of togetherness. The celebration brought students and teachers together to embrace the spirit of the festival through cultural participation, creativity and shared moments of celebration. The occasion reflected the values of light, positivity and harmony while providing students an opportunity to connect with the rich cultural traditions associated with Diwali.
