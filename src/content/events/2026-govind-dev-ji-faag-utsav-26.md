On 25 February 2026, students of Fankaar Institute of Performing Arts offered their Kathak hajiri at the divine lotus feet of Govind Dev Ji. The presentation was an offering of devotion through the traditional language of Kathak, allowing the students to present their practice in a sacred and spiritual setting.

The hajiri brought together rhythm, graceful movement and expressive storytelling, reflecting the students’ dedication to their Kathak training. Through their presentation, they paid their respects to Govind Dev Ji while continuing the cherished tradition of offering dance as a form of devotion.
