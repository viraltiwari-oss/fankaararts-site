Fankaar Institute of Performing Arts celebrated Diwali on 29 October 2024 with warmth, joy and a beautiful spirit of togetherness. The celebration brought students and teachers together to embrace the festive season through cultural participation, creativity and shared moments of happiness.

The occasion reflected the essence of Diwali—light, positivity, harmony and community—while creating a meaningful space for students to connect with the rich cultural traditions and festive spirit of India. It was a memorable celebration filled with warmth, creativity and the joy of coming together as a Fankaar family.
