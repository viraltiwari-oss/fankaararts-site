Fankaar Institute of Performing Arts presented a vibrant Kathak performance at the Govind Dev Ji Phag Utsav, celebrating the colours and festive spirit of Faag through dance, music and devotion. The presentation captured the joyous essence of the festival, bringing alive the playful and devotional world of Radha-Krishna through expressive Kathak.

Through graceful movements, intricate footwork and expressive abhinaya, the performance reflected the celebration of Faag while beautifully blending rhythm, storytelling and devotion. The presentation created a lively festive atmosphere and celebrated the rich cultural tradition associated with Govind Dev Ji and the colours of Holi.
