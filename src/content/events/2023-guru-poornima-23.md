Guru Poornima 2023 was celebrated at Fankaar Institute of Performing Arts on 3 July 2023, honouring the sacred bond between the Guru and disciple. The occasion was a heartfelt expression of gratitude towards the Gurus whose guidance, knowledge and blessings shape an artist’s journey.

Through Kathak, music and collective remembrance, the celebration reflected the essence of the Guru–Shishya Parampara, celebrating tradition, discipline, devotion and the timeless knowledge passed down through generations.
