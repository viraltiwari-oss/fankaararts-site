Guru Poornima 2026 at Fankaar was a heartfelt celebration dedicated to the sacred bond between the Guru and disciple. The occasion brought together students to express their respect, gratitude and heartfelt regards towards their Gurus, acknowledging their guidance and contribution to their artistic journey.

Through this celebration, students came together to honour the teachings, values and traditions passed down by their Gurus. It was a meaningful occasion that reflected the spirit of gratitude, devotion and the timeless Guru-Shishya tradition.
