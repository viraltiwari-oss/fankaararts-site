Fankaar Institute of Performing Arts organised Nritya Samaroha on 21 June 2024, marking the culmination of its Beginner Kathak Workshop. The event brought together the workshop participants and Fankaar students in a celebratory presentation that reflected their learning, practice and engagement with the art of Kathak.

The programme featured Ganesh Vandana and Shuddha Kathak presented by the workshop participants, while Fankaar students presented Shudha Kathak and Ram Stuti as part of the evening’s repertoire. The event celebrated the discipline of riyaaz, the joy of learning and the expressive beauty of Kathak, bringing the workshop to a meaningful conclusion.
