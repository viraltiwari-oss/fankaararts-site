On the first anniversary of Jaipur Centre for Culture and Arts (JCCA), Fankaar Institute of Performing Arts students presented a graceful Kathak performance celebrating devotion, rhythm and the rich tradition of Indian classical dance. The presentation began with Ram Vandana, portraying devotion through expressive movements and abhinaya.

The performance was followed by Shudha Kathak, showcasing intricate footwork, rhythmic patterns, chakkars and traditional Kathak compositions. The presentation reflected the students’ dedication, training and growing confidence, making the occasion a memorable celebration of art and culture.
