Fankaar Institute of Performing Arts celebrated Diwali on 18 October 2025 with warmth, joy and a beautiful spirit of togetherness. The celebration brought students and teachers together to welcome the festival of lights through cultural participation, creativity and shared moments of happiness.

The occasion reflected the essence of Diwali—light, positivity, harmony and community—while providing students with an opportunity to celebrate India’s rich cultural traditions in a joyful and meaningful atmosphere. It was a memorable celebration filled with festive spirit and togetherness.
