Fankaar Institute of Performing Arts conducted a six-day Beginner Kathak Workshop at its Nirman Nagar Branch from 15 to 20 June 2024, led by Vartika Tiwari, Founder-Director of Fankaar Institute of Performing Arts. The workshop was designed to introduce beginners to the foundational vocabulary of Kathak through a structured and engaging learning experience.

Participants were introduced to essential elements of Kathak, including posture, tatkaar, hastak, rhythm and basic expressions, while developing coordination, musicality and an understanding of the discipline of the form. The workshop offered beginners a strong foundation to begin their Kathak journey with confidence, curiosity and a deeper appreciation for this classical art form.
