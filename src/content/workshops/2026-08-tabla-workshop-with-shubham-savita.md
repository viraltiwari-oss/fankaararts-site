Fankaar conducted an exclusive Tabla Workshop for its students on 20 June 2026, led by Shubham Savita. The workshop focused on helping students develop a stronger understanding of rhythm and taal and build a deeper connection between dance and percussion.

Students were introduced to the basic techniques of playing tabla and learned simple thekas, giving them practical experience and a better understanding of how rhythm supports their Kathak practice.
