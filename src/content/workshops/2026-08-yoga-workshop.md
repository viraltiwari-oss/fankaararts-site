Fankaar Institute of Performing Arts hosted an exclusive Yoga Workshop on 7 June 2026 at its Tonk Road Branch, led by Surgeet Kaur. Curated exclusively for Fankaar students, the workshop explored yoga as a complementary practice for dancers, with a focus on improving flexibility, balance, strength, body alignment and mindful movement.

The session introduced students to yoga poses and practices that can support a Kathak dancer’s physical conditioning and movement quality. It included guided warm-up and cooldown practices, along with exercises designed to enhance mobility, stability and body awareness. The workshop also highlighted how a consistent yoga practice can contribute to better posture, controlled movement, balance and overall physical preparedness for dance training.
