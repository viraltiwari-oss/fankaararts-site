Fankaar conducted a four-day Folk Dance Workshop from 24th to 27th June 2026, led by Sangeeta Sain. The workshop introduced students to the rich tradition of Rajasthani folk dance and its cultural significance.

Students learned about Ghoomar and other elements of Rajasthani folk dance through practical training, gaining an opportunity to explore the vibrant movements, rhythms, and traditions of Rajasthan.
