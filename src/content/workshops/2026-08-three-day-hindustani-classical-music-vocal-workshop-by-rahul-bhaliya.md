Fankaar Institute of Performing Arts conducted a three-day Vocal Music Workshop from 7th to 9th June 2026, conducted by Mr. Rahul Bhaliya Ji.

The workshop introduced students to the basics of Hindustani Classical Music, including the understanding of Swaras — Sa, Re, Ga, Ma, Pa and their role in music. Students also explored the practical connection between music and Kathak, learning how to play Kathak Lehra on the harmonium. The workshop gave students an opportunity to understand the musical foundation behind their dance practice while developing a deeper appreciation of Indian classical music.
