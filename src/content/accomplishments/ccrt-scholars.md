 Bhavika Garg, a dedicated Kathak student of Fankaar Institute of Performing Arts, was selected for the prestigious CCRT Junior Scholarship by the Centre for Cultural Resources and Training (CCRT), Ministry of Culture, Government of India.

Among numerous applicants from Rajasthan, Bhavika was selected as one of only four scholarship recipients from the state and was the first and only candidate from Fankaar Institute of Performing Arts to receive this recognition. Her selection reflects her dedication, discipline, perseverance and commitment to the classical art form of Kathak.

This achievement marks a significant milestone in Bhavika’s artistic journey and is a proud moment for her Gurus, family and Fankaar Institute of Performing Arts.
