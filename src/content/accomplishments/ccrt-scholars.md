Bhavika Garg, a dedicated Kathak student of Fankaar Arts, was awarded the prestigious CCRT (Centre for Cultural Resources and Training) Junior Scholarship in 2023 for her outstanding dedication and commitment to Kathak. Among numerous applicants from Rajasthan, Bhavika was one of the four recipients selected for this esteemed national-level scholarship.

Her selection marked a significant milestone in her artistic journey and was a proud moment for the entire Fankaar Institute of Performing Arts. The achievement reflects her consistent practice, perseverance, discipline and passion for the art form.

The recognition also celebrates the invaluable support of her family and mentors, whose guidance and encouragement have been an integral part of her growth as a Kathak dancer. Bhavika’s achievement stands as an inspiration for aspiring young artists and reflects the spirit of dedication and excellence that Fankaar Institute of Performing Arts strives to nurture.
