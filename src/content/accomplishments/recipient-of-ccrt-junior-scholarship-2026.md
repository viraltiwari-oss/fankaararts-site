Nirvi Ajmera, a dedicated and hardworking student of Fankaar Institute of Performing Arts, was awarded the prestigious CCRT Scholarship for her sincere dedication and growing excellence in the field of Indian classical dance. She is among the four students from Rajasthan who were selected for this esteemed scholarship, making this achievement a significant milestone in her artistic journey.

Known for her discipline, perseverance and passion for Kathak, Nirvi has consistently demonstrated a strong commitment to her learning and practice. Her sincere efforts, regular dedication and enthusiasm towards the art form reflect her determination to grow as a young Kathak practitioner.

This recognition is a proud moment for Nirvi as well as Fankaar Institute of Performing Arts. The CCRT Scholarship acknowledges her potential and dedication while encouraging her to continue her journey of learning, exploration and excellence in Indian classical dance.
