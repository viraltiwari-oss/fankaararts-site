Vanshika Hotani, a dedicated and talented Kathak student of Fankaar Institute of Performing Arts, was selected for the prestigious FCDFO Scholarship by the Federation of Classical Dance Festival Organisers (FCDFO). This recognition marks an important milestone in her artistic journey and reflects her sincere dedication, consistent practice and commitment to the discipline of Kathak.

The scholarship acknowledges Vanshika’s potential and her continued efforts towards developing her skills in Indian classical dance. Her selection is a proud achievement for both the student and the institute, highlighting the value of perseverance, rigorous training and passion for the art form.

Fankaar Institute of Performing Arts takes immense pride in celebrating Vanshika Hotani’s achievement and congratulates her on this significant recognition. The scholarship serves as an encouragement for her to continue pursuing excellence in Kathak and to further contribute to the rich tradition of Indian classical dance.
